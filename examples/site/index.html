What the window costs

A million rows is
the same amount of DOM
as thirty.

The renderer keeps a window of rows and a window of columns — two Fenwick trees and a node pool — so the DOM stops growing at the size of the viewport and stays there.

FHIR, with the awkward parts

Opaque link.next carried verbatim. Bundle.total that may be absent, an estimate, or a lie — reported as "unknown" rather than a number. _count negotiated down. _include entries that are context, not rows. A filter that cannot be expressed as search runs nothing and says which part it could not compile.

Disclosure that reaches every output

One call applies the policy to the renderer, CSV, XLSX, the print sheet, the clipboard and a transaction that patches a row later. A contract test asserts a masked value does not appear in the bytes of any of them.

Errors that cannot leak

A grid error carries a code, a phase, a column and a row index. No message, no stack, no cause, no row id — a row key is commonly an MRN. What a log can hold is decided by the type, not by a reviewer's attention.

Coverage, stated or refused

Every set says what it reached and what it did not. A source that timed out is named in the coverage sentence, and that sentence travels into the CSV and prints in the running footer — paper is where "see all" stops existing.
